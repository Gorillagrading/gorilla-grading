# PROTOTYPE MODELS

The purpose is to create multiple visual/product directions before committing to one.

## MODEL 01 — PRECISION LAB

### Concept
Gorilla as a precision technology company.

### Feel
- clean
- technical
- controlled
- premium
- quiet

### Visual
- black/off-white base
- Gorilla green as accent
- generous whitespace
- strong typography
- precise diagrams
- close-up card imagery

### Hero
Large card scan on the right/center, with subtle technical overlays.

### Signature interaction
“See how we grade” — reveal measurements and evidence layer by layer.

### Best for
Explaining technology and building perceived rigor.

---

## MODEL 02 — COLLECTOR GALLERY

### Concept
The slab and the card as collectible objects.

### Feel
- luxury
- editorial
- museum/gallery
- emotional
- sophisticated

### Visual
- warm off-white / charcoal
- Gorilla green used sparingly
- large card photography
- elegant typography
- restrained motion

### Hero
Single hero card treated like an art object.

### Signature interaction
Scroll through “Raw → Graded → Certified”.

### Best for
Connecting with emotional collectors and premium positioning.

---

## MODEL 03 — DIGITAL JUNGLE

### Concept
Keep the seriousness of grading while giving Gorilla more personality.

### Feel
- modern
- energetic but controlled
- community-driven
- distinctive

### Visual
- Gorilla green
- charcoal
- off-white
- very subtle jungle/palm textures
- banana only in informal areas

### Hero
Card + Gorilla identity + dynamic scanning animation.

### Signature interaction
Live “Card Journey” animation.

### Best for
Social/community and younger TCG audience.

---

## MODEL 04 — HYBRID / RECOMMENDED BASELINE FOR TESTING

Combine:
- Model 01's technical clarity;
- Model 02's premium card presentation;
- Model 03's personality in controlled doses.

This is a prototype direction, not a final decision.
